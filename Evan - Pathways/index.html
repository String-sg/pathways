🎯 SEC 2028 Minimum Entry Requirements (MER)

Adapted for Secondary 2 students based on MOE's official SEC 2028 admissions framework. Eligibility shows whether general admission criteria are met. Specific courses also require course-specific prerequisites and cut-off points.

* Footnote on Pass/Fail Simplification for Sec 2s: Results in this explorer are simplified to Pass/Fail so you can focus on how your subject levels (G1/G2/G3) unlock post-secondary doors without worrying about exact numerical points yet. In the actual SEC examination:
• Mother Tongue at G1: Grade A to D satisfies the subject prerequisite for Junior College and Millennia Institute (students offer MTL 'B' at pre-U). G1 MTL cannot be included in L1R4, ELR2B2, or PFP aggregates.
• PFP requires strong passes: Grade 1–3 for English & Maths, and Grade 1–4 for other subjects (scraped Grade 5 passes do not qualify for PFP).
• Polytechnic B2 subject: Must be at least G2 Grade 4 or G3 A1–E8 (Grade 5/6 not accepted for B2 in ELR2B2).
• Prerequisites Met vs. Final Posting: Meeting prerequisites means you are eligible to apply; final posting is competitive and depends on meeting the course aggregate score cut-off (e.g. JC gross L1R4 ≤ 16, Poly net ELR2B2 ≤ 22, PFP gross ELMAB3 ≤ 12, ITE gross ELMAB3 ≤ 19).

🩺 Nursing Career Pathway: Enrolled Nurse to Registered Nurse & Degree

If a student qualifies for the 3-Year Higher Nitec pathway, Singapore offers a structured, progressive career ladder from an Enrolled Nurse (EN) up to a Registered Nurse (RN) and a University Degree. No student is locked out of a nursing career!

Stage 1 • Duration: 3 Years

ITE 3-Year Higher Nitec in Nursing

Campus: ITE College East (School of Health Sciences)

  • Year 1: Broad-based foundational healthcare and biological science modules.
  • Years 2 & 3: Core nursing specialization, high-tech simulation ward practice, and extensive hospital postings.
  • Outcome: Graduate with Higher Nitec in Nursing; eligible to register with Singapore Nursing Board (SNB) to practise as a licensed Enrolled Nurse (EN).
Stage 2 • Duration: 2 to 3 Years

Diploma in Nursing → Registered Nurse (RN)

Two distinct pathways to upgrade to a Registered Nurse (RN):

  • Route A: Full-time Polytechnic Diploma at NYP or NP (2 to 3 years). With GPA ≥ 3.0, graduates qualify for advanced placement into an accelerated 2-year or 2.5-year route.
  • Route B: ITE Work-Study Diploma (WSDip) in Nursing (3 years). Work as an Enrolled Nurse earning full salary while fully sponsored for part-time diploma training!
Stage 3 • Duration: 2 to 3 Years

University Degree in Nursing (Optional)

Institutions: National University of Singapore (NUS) or Singapore Institute of Technology (SIT).

  • Programme: Bachelor of Science in Nursing.
  • Duration: 2 to 3 years (diploma graduates receive credit exemptions).
  • Outcome: Direct progression to Senior Staff Nurse, Nurse Clinician, Nurse Educator, or Advanced Practice Nurse (APN).

Glossary of Terms

SEC
Singapore-Cambridge Secondary Education Certificate (taken at the end of Secondary 4 from 2027/2028).
G1, G2, G3
General 1, 2, and 3 under Full SBB. G3 is the most demanding (Express standard).
G1 Mother Tongue
Grade A to D satisfies the subject entry requirement for JC and MI (students will offer MTL 'B'). It cannot be computed into L1R4, ELR2B2, or PFP ELMAB3, but counts 1–4 points for ITE 3-Year Higher Nitec.
MTL 'B'
A pre-university Mother Tongue syllabus at JC and MI with greater emphasis on practical listening and spoken skills, offered to students who took G1 Mother Tongue in secondary school.
L1R4
First Language + 4 Relevant subjects (all at G3). Used for Junior College and Millennia Institute.
ELR2B2
English + 2 Relevant + Best 2 other subjects. Used for Polytechnic Year 1 diplomas (ELR2B2-A/B/C/D).
ELMAB3
English + Mathematics + Best 3 other subjects. Used for PFP (≤12) and 2-Year Higher Nitec (≤19).
PFP
Polytechnic Foundation Programme: a 1-year foundation leading directly to a poly diploma without a 5th year in secondary school.
Gross vs Net
JC (≤16) and PFP (≤12) require gross scores before CCA deduction. Poly Year 1 (≤22) is net after deducting up to 2 CCA bonus points.
Higher Nitec
ITE certificate leading to employment or poly progression. 2-Year uses ELMAB3; 3-Year uses R2B2, R1B3, or B4.
HMTL
Higher Mother Tongue Language (G3). Passing both G3 English and HMTL awards 2 bonus points for JC admission (capped at 3 total bonus points).
Science at our school
Our school offers Double Science (split into 2: Chemistry, Physics, Biology) and Combined Science. Triple Science is not offered.
Computing & Business
Our school offers Computing across G1, G2, and G3 levels. Computing directly qualifies as a relevant subject for Polytechnic Information & Digital Technologies diplomas (ELR2B2-C), ITE Infocomm courses (R1B3-B), and counts towards JC/MI L1R4. Business (EBS) is offered at G1 level.
CourseFinder
Official MOE directory (moe.gov.sg/coursefinder) where students can check entry requirements for specific diploma & Higher Nitec courses including Computing.
Enrolled Nurse (EN)
A licensed healthcare professional registered with the Singapore Nursing Board (SNB) via the ITE 3-Year Higher Nitec in Nursing (College East). ENs deliver vital patient care and can upgrade to Registered Nurse via Polytechnic Diploma or ITE Work-Study Diploma.
Registered Nurse (RN)
A professional nurse registered with SNB after completing a Polytechnic Diploma in Nursing, ITE Work-Study Diploma, or University Degree. RNs develop patient care plans, administer complex medications, and supervise nursing teams.
Work-Study Diploma (Nursing)
A 3-year fully sponsored upgrading diploma by ITE in partnership with public healthcare clusters. Enrolled Nurses work full-time earning a regular monthly salary while completing part-time diploma training to upgrade to Registered Nurses.
